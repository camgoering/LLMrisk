Based on Google's Privacy Policy effective October 16, 2026, there is no fixed annual dollar cost to accepting it. The main financial risks are indirect: targeted advertising influencing your spending, potential exposure of personal information, and the consequences of a data breach.

If you want a single dollar figure for personal budgeting, I'd use $100 per year as a provisional risk allowance for an average user—not as a statistically established expected loss.

That number is a planning assumption, not a charge from Google or a value stated in the policy. Your actual financial impact could be $0, considerably higher, or even a net benefit because of the services you receive.

## How I'd assess the financial risk

The main provisions that create potential financial exposure are:

| Risk from the policy                               | Potential financial consequence                             |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personalized advertising based on your activity    | Additional spending influenced by targeted ads              |
| Collection of location, browsing and purchase data | Potential privacy loss and misuse if information is exposed |
| Storage of emails, documents and photos            | Financial harm if sensitive information is compromised      |
| Sharing with processors and legal authorities      | Possible indirect costs from information disclosure         |
| Long-term data retention                           | Continued exposure of stored information                    |

None of these provisions establishes a measurable annual loss for an individual.

For context, the FTC reported a $497 median loss among fraud reports in 2024, but that statistic covers fraud generally—not fraud caused by Google or its privacy policy.&#x20;

## My recommendation

I would not reject Google's services solely on financial-risk grounds. Instead, I'd accept the policy only with privacy settings adjusted to minimize unnecessary collection and use of personal information.

Start with [Google Privacy Checkup](https://myaccount.google.com/privacycheckup?utm_source=chatgpt.com) and [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) to review your settings.

Bottom line: $100 annually is a usable hypothetical budget allowance, but the evidence does not establish that accepting this policy actually costs you $100—or any other specific amount—each year. The most important risk may be loss of privacy rather than direct financial loss.
