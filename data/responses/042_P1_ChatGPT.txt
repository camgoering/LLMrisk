**Privacy risk grade: C (moderate). Annual dollar figure: $10/year as an illustrative scenario, not a measured estimate.** That figure assumes a 1% annual chance of a privacy incident that costs you $1,000. The policy provides neither the odds nor the likely cost, so it doesn’t support a reliable estimate of your actual yearly loss. The dollar figure also doesn’t capture harms like embarrassment or loss of confidentiality. 

I’d give it a **C for ordinary personal use** because the policy allows collection of prompts, uploads, and account and device data; disclosure to service providers and, in specified circumstances, authorities or other third parties; and retention beyond deletion in some cases. On the other hand, it offers training and marketing opt-outs, and says Temporary Chats aren’t used for training and are generally deleted within 30 days, subject to safety or legal exceptions.      

I’d raise that to **D (high)** if you routinely upload identifiable medical, financial, or confidential work records. The most useful precaution is to redact sensitive details before uploading: turning off training limits one use of your content, but it doesn’t stop collection or eliminate retention exceptions.
