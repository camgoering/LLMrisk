I’d grade X’s privacy policy **D (high risk)** for a typical personal account. **$50 per year** is an illustrative amount I’d use to weigh the privacy trade-off against the value you get from X. It’s not a fee or a forecast of financial loss. The policy doesn’t provide the odds or costs of privacy harms needed to calculate a reliable yearly loss.

The D reflects the breadth of collection and what can happen to the data. X says it collects the contents of standard Direct Messages and metadata for Encrypted Messages, can combine off-platform ad-partner activity with other information about you, and may use collected and publicly available information to train AI models. Some third-party collaborators may also use shared information for their own AI training if you don’t opt out. Public posts can persist elsewhere even after you delete them from X.
