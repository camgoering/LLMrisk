I give it a **C** because Netflix collects detailed viewing and search history, device identifiers, and payment information. It also uses identifiers and general location for targeted advertising, and says some marketing and advertising practices qualify as “sale” or “sharing” under certain U.S. state privacy laws. The policy describes ways to limit those practices, but the opt-outs don’t stop all collection or undo prior sharing.   

If you accept, start with **Account → Privacy and data settings** for each profile. Turn off **Behavioral Advertising** where available and review **Matched Identifier Communications** to limit Netflix marketing that matches your account to third-party services.
