I’d give it a **C** because Netflix collects detailed viewing and search activity, device identifiers, and advertising information that can include interests inferred from activity on other sites and apps. Its U.S. notice also says certain uses of identifiers for marketing and targeted ads can count as “sale” or “sharing” under some state laws. The policy offers opt-outs, but the “Do Not Sell or Share” choice doesn’t undo prior sales or stop all behavioral advertising.   

If you accept, the first thing I’d do is open **Account → Privacy and data settings** for each profile and turn off **Behavioral Advertising** where available.
