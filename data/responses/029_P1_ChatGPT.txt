## What creates financial risk?

The policy permits several activities that are relevant to your financial exposure:

- Collecting personal information: Names, account details, transaction history, device information, and potentially sensitive conversation content.
- Using your conversations: Content may be used for model improvement, depending on your settings.
- Sharing information: Data may be disclosed to vendors, certain marketing partners, government authorities in specified circumstances, and other parties.
- Retaining information: Some information may be retained after deletion for legal, security, or other stated reasons.
- Security limitations: OpenAI acknowledges that no internet transmission is completely secure.

These provisions create potential privacy exposure, but none establishes the probability that you personally will suffer a financial loss.

If you require one number for a personal risk-planning exercise, $10 per year is a possible hypothetical allowance under the assumptions shown above. It is not an independently verified estimate of the risk caused by accepting OpenAI's policy.

For someone who uses ChatGPT only for general questions, the financial exposure may be relatively limited. Someone who enters banking details, tax documents, confidential business information, or other sensitive material could have substantially greater exposure.

To reduce the risks permitted by the policy, consider disabling model-training use of your conversations under Settings → Data Controls, avoiding unnecessary sensitive uploads, and using Temporary Chat when appropriate.
